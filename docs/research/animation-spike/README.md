# Animation spike (Sep 30, 2026)

A throwaway Godot project, built in scratch before tasks 7 and 14, that tested whether weapon paths can animate the Quaternius fighters: one fighter, the Katana, locomotion, a guard and two cuts.

- `report.md`: what was built and the verdict (yes, with conditions), with measurements.
- `critique.md`: the review of its screenshots. Its numbered fixes and conditions are requirements for plan tasks 7, 14, 14b and 15.
- `code/`: the spike's scripts and shaders, kept as the starting point the plan names. `src/swing.gd`, `attack_driver.gd`, `rig.gd` and `body_layer.gd` matter most. It is not part of the game: nothing loads it, and the screenshots and imported assets it refers to were not kept.
