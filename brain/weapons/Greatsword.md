# Greatsword

A [[Weapon class|colossal]] weapon: slow and crushing, with high HP and posture damage and big knockback. The fighter rides its momentum, and every grounded swing except the bashes ends with a short slide (0.35 m over 10 frames). Its duelling distance is 3.0 m, its parry window 0.20 s, and a blocker takes 60% of its posture damage. Its wielder moves 10% slower.

## Moves

- **Light string:** Heavy Swing (right to left, stepping in) → Backswing (left to right, starting faster because it rides the momentum).
- **Heavies:** Overhead Strike (chargeable) → Low Sweep, an [[Unblockable]] sweep at the feet that can be jumped.
- **Dodge attacks:** Piercing Lunge (a blockable stab) and Skewer (an unblockable thrust).
- **[[Block ability|Block abilities]]** (pick two): Reaping Sweep (unblockable), Mountain Slam (an unblockable overhead) and Guard Crusher (a shoulder bash that deals heavy posture damage even when blocked).
- **[[Ultimate]], Impaler:** the fighter takes aim, dashes across the stage and impales the opponent. Pressing heavy while they're impaled detonates a burst of energy.
