# Hand-to-hand

What every fighter fights with once [[Disarm|disarmed]]: their [[Bare hands]]. The fighter becomes more agile (moving 20% faster, dodging 50% farther, jumping 35% higher), but can't block or parry. The duelling distance is 1.85 m (1.6 m until the bodies grew taller).

- **Moves:** a jab–cross–hook chain, roundhouse and spinning-heel kicks, a flying knee out of a sprint, and an axe kick from a jump. Hits do little HP damage but high posture damage and extra knockback. The light string is made in Cascadeur from key poses (milestone-1 task 89): the Jab, the Cross and the Hook land 18, 20 and 22 frames after the press and take the frozen light hitstun, so no hit of the string is guaranteed any more. The heavies followed (task 133): a true roundhouse, its key poses authored by script since the packs hold only a front kick and a spinning kick, its held charge looping a pose loaded on the back foot, and a full spinning back heel; they land 36 and 40 frames after the press.
- **[[Redirect]]** replaces the [[Parry]]: tapping block on impact deals 35 posture damage and stuns. It disarms an armed attacker whose posture is full.
- **[[Ultimate]]:** Recall (the weapon tears out of the ground and flies back to the hand, and the burst blasts a near opponent away) or Breaker Palm (a crouch, a surge of about 3 m and a rising palm: a big posture blow).

The design wants each fighter to have their own bare-hand style; for now they all share one. [[Disarm and re-arm]] covers getting the weapon back.
