# Hand-to-hand

What every fighter fights with once [[Disarm|disarmed]]: their [[Bare hands]]. The fighter becomes more agile (moving 20% faster, dodging 50% farther, jumping 35% higher), but can't block or parry. The duelling distance is 1.6 m.

- **Moves:** a jab–cross–hook chain, roundhouse and spinning-heel kicks, a flying knee out of a sprint, and an axe kick from a jump. Hits do little HP damage but high posture damage and extra knockback. The first two lights keep their own 16-frame hitstun, so the early hits stay guaranteed.
- **[[Redirect]]** replaces the [[Parry]]: tapping block on impact deals 35 posture damage and stuns. It disarms an armed attacker whose posture is full.
- **[[Ultimate]]:** Recall (the weapon flies back to the hand) or Breaker Palm (a big posture blow).

The design wants each fighter to have their own bare-hand style; for now they all share one. [[Disarm and re-arm]] covers getting the weapon back.
