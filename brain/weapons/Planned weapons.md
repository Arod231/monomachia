# Planned weapons

The design doc names nine weapons, and three are built: the [[Katana]], the [[Greatsword]] and the [[Twin Daggers]]. The other six belong to the full game and aren't in the rebuild's plan yet.

| Weapon | Class | Idea | Ultimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Odachi | Colossal | A giant katana: slow and powerful, with longer range and a larger parry window. Overhead slam, sweep and area spin abilities | A rising slash that launches the enemy, then a slash as they fall |
| Giant Hammer | Colossal | High posture and HP damage and knockback. Side-to-side swings into a golf swing, and a forward-flip slam | A spinning approach and a golf swing that hurls the enemy into a wall |
| Staff | Medium | Fast, with low damage and good range. Thrust, sweep and spin abilities; spinning is its identity | Sits atop the staff like Sun Wukong, then slams down |
| Longsword & Buckler | Medium | All-round, and the shield gives good blocking. Thrust abilities and a shield bash that stuns | Jumps high and slams the shield down |
| Bladed Whip | Small | Fast, long range, mid damage. The whip extends straight after a telegraphed windup | Coils up, then spins a whip strike across the stage |
| Scythe | Colossal | Reaping side-to-side strikes, an overhead slam, an unblockable sweep and an auto-parry spin | Leaps high and sweeps down horizontally |

Colossal weapons should be big two-handed weapons, as in Elden Ring, that drag the fighter along with their momentum. See [[Weapon class]].
