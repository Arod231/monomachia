# Camera

Over the right shoulder, like For Honor but a little more zoomed out, and always locked on to the opponent: this is always a duel.

- About 4.6 m back and 1.3–1.4 m to the side when the fighters stand 3.5 m apart or more. It swings out by 0.8 m for each metre closer (to about 3.5 m at the closest), so the player never hides the opponent.
- Low enough that blades read against the sky, with a 60° field of view.
- On the [[Moonlit Shrine]] it stays within 16.2 m of the centre, so with a fighter backed against the wall it comes in to about 1.6 m behind them.
- A parry gives a brief camera push-in, and hits shake it and kick its field of view. Settings has "Reduce flashes and shaking": it cuts the shake to 0.15, drops the field-of-view kicks (and the push-in, once milestone 1 adds it), and dims the flashes, the fighters' body flashes included, to 0.45 at full size. It applies at once, even from the pause menu.
- Watch films side-on, and Versus gives each player their own camera in split screen.

There's no free camera yet. The camera rig is presentation only: it reads the rules and never changes them ([[Presentation layer]]).
