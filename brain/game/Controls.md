# Controls

Every action can be remapped for keyboard and mouse and for controller, and saved in named profiles. PlayStation and Xbox controllers show their own button names.

| Action | Keyboard & mouse | Controller (PlayStation / Xbox) |
|---|---|---|
| Move around the opponent | W A S D | Left stick / D-pad |
| [[Light attack]] | Left click or J | R1 / RB |
| [[Heavy attack]] (hold to charge) | Right click or K | R2 / RT |
| [[Block]] / [[Parry]] | Shift or L | L1 / LB |
| Dodge / backstep | Space | ○ / B |
| Jump | F or I | ✕ / A |
| [[Ultimate]] | Q or U, or light + heavy | △ / Y, or both attacks |
| [[Block ability]] 1 / 2 | Hold block + light / heavy | Hold L1/LB + R1/R2 or RB/RT |
| Pick up weapon | E | □ / X |
| Pause | Esc or P | Options / Menu |

Tap a direction to step; double-tap and hold to sprint. Moving up and down goes toward and away from the opponent, and left and right circle them.

In Versus each player picks a device, and two people can share one keyboard: the arrow-key layout uses arrows to move, with J K L for light, heavy and block.

The rebuild keeps the demo's defaults and remapping, saved per profile on the PC. The input layer reads devices through an interface, so tests can fake them.
