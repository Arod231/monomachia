# Unblockables and counters

[[Unblockable|Unblockables]] are slow, telegraphed attacks. Blocking one takes the full hit (and disarms a blocker whose posture is full), and dodge invincibility doesn't avoid it. During the wind-up a red 危 ("danger") mark and the attack type show above the attacker, and the blade glows red. They reach 10 cm further than the same swing would (`UNBLOCKABLE_SWEEP_BONUS`).

Each kind has its own [[Counter]], and any of them can also be parried:

| Attack | Counter | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Thrust | **Stomp:** dodge *into* it, like Sekiro's mikiri counter | The attacker is stunned and open to a string |
| Sweep | **Leap:** jump over it | The defender vaults off the attacker for big posture damage |
| Overhead slam | **Evade:** back-dash from it | A quick lunge strike while the attacker recovers |

The counters are deliberately generous: they keep wide cone checks even now that real [[Weapon swings]] decide ordinary hits.

Examples: the Katana's Piercing Thrust and Swallow Sweep, the Greatsword's Low Sweep, Skewer, Reaping Sweep and Mountain Slam, and the Daggers' Serpent Sweep and Needle Thrust. See [[Katana]], [[Greatsword]] and [[Twin Daggers]].
