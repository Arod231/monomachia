# Ultimates

An [[Ultimate]] unlocks at 25% HP or less, once per round, and while it's ready embers rise off the fighter, a heat haze shimmers round them and a faint rim glows in their side's colour. Pressing light and heavy together fires it. Blocking one with a full posture meter disarms the blocker.

| Weapon | Ultimate | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [[Katana]] | Moonsplitter | Sheathe, hold a crouched stance, then draw: a slash across the whole stage, vertical (step aside) or horizontal (jump it), leaving 60 frames in, the stick picking until the draw starts. The wave reaches 2 × the arena radius + 3 m |
| [[Greatsword]] | Impaler | Aim, dash across the stage and impale; press heavy to detonate a burst. The dash ends 0.7 m inside the wall |
| [[Twin Daggers]] | Lightning Tempest | Dash in and spin through six strikes, each parriable |
| [[Hand-to-hand]] | Recall or Breaker Palm | From a gathering stance: roar and call the weapon, which tears out of the ground and arcs back to the hand while a burst blasts a near opponent 2.0 m back onto the floor; or crouch, surge about 3 m and drive a rising palm (36 / 4 / 36, touching from 4.1 m) for a big posture blow |

Ultimates are scripted hits: they keep their own hit checks rather than the [[Weapon swings]], and their effects land halfway between the fighters at 1.25 m. Moonsplitter's wave is a crescent of moonlight, silver at its edge and violet behind, lighting the floor as it flies and shedding mist and petals; the choice lifts haze and petals, Breaker Palm lands with a pale-gold flash and a ring of distortion, and the recall's burst rolls a larger ring. A wind-up stays on the gameplay camera with a roar and a slow push-in, and Moonsplitter and Breaker Palm play a cinematic shot once they connect. [[Planned weapons]] lists the design's ultimates for the six weapons still to come.
