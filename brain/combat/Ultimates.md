# Ultimates

An [[Ultimate]] unlocks at 25% HP or less, once per round, and the fighter glows while it's ready. Pressing light and heavy together fires it. Blocking one with a full posture meter disarms the blocker.

| Weapon | Ultimate | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [[Katana]] | Moonsplitter | Sheathe, then a slash across the whole stage: vertical (step aside) or horizontal (jump it). The wave reaches 2 × the arena radius + 3 m |
| [[Greatsword]] | Impaler | Aim, dash across the stage and impale; press heavy to detonate a burst. The dash ends 0.7 m inside the wall |
| [[Twin Daggers]] | Lightning Tempest | Dash in and spin through six strikes, each parriable |
| [[Hand-to-hand]] | Recall or Breaker Palm | Bring the weapon back, or land a big posture blow |

Ultimates are scripted hits: they keep their own hit checks rather than the [[Weapon swings]], and their effects land halfway between the fighters at 1.25 m. [[Planned weapons]] lists the design's ultimates for the six weapons still to come.
