# Posture system

[[Posture]] is the 100-point meter under the HP bar. It fills under pressure, and a full meter is the danger state.

## What fills it

- Blocking: a reduced share of the attack's posture value (60–80%, by weapon).
- Getting hit: 1.5× the attack's posture value.
- Getting parried: 16.
- Getting countered: 15–35. A [[Redirect]] deals 35.

## What drains it

Posture drains only while the fighter holds block and has taken no posture damage for 0.75 s: about 14 points a second standing still and 5 while moving, at full HP. Lower HP slows the drain, down to 35% of normal near 0 HP.

## When it's full

The meter flashes. While it's full, having an attack parried [[Disarm|disarms]] the fighter, and so does trying to block an [[Unblockable]], a full-charge power attack or an [[Ultimate]]. The disarming blow does no HP damage, and posture resets to empty once the weapon flies. See [[Disarm and re-arm]].

The design's intent: keep up the pressure, because every attack fills the defender's meter, but blocking too much fills your own, while parrying fills the attacker's.
