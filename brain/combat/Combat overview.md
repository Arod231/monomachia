# Combat overview

Two fighters face off, locked on to each other, in a walled [[Arena]]. Each has 100 HP and a 100-point [[Posture]] meter. Emptying the opponent's HP wins the [[Round]], and the first to win three rounds wins the [[Match]].

The loop:

1. **Pressure.** [[Attacking|Attacks]] ([[Light attack|lights]], [[Heavy attack|heavies]], [[String|strings]], [[Movement attack|movement attacks]], [[Block ability|block abilities]]) wear down HP. Every attack fills the defender's posture, whether it hits or is blocked.
2. **Defend.** [[Defending|Block]] to protect HP at a posture cost, [[Parry]] with precise timing to fill the attacker's posture instead, dodge through attacks, or jump sweeps. [[Unblockables and counters|Unblockables]] go through a block and have their own counters.
3. **Break.** A fighter with a full meter is in danger: having an attack parried, or blocking an unblockable, a power attack or an ultimate, [[Disarm and re-arm|disarms]] them.
4. **Fight on bare-handed.** The disarmed fighter fights [[Hand-to-hand]] and tries to get their weapon back, while the other tries to stand in the way.
5. **Finish.** At 25% HP or less a fighter gets one [[Ultimates|ultimate]] per round.

Holding block while not under attack drains posture, fastest when standing still: the [[Posture system]] rewards pausing to recover.

What the game deliberately leaves out: directional guard (block is one hold, not For Honor's stances) and combo breakers. Defence happens before a hit lands; the rebuild's lighter hitstun means later string hits can still be blocked or parried ([[Task 8]]).
