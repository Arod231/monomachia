# Monomachia: the second brain

Monomachia is a one-on-one weapon duel: two fighters with a weapon each, and the first to win three rounds takes the match. It takes inspiration from For Honor, Tekken, Sekiro and Soul Calibur, and it's being rebuilt in Godot. This vault holds the project's knowledge as linked notes; [[About this vault]] explains how it's built.
